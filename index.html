Our Story

How We Met

Some love stories start in coffee shops. Others begin at sunset on a beach. Ours? Ours started on the Rift.

Stephen was locked in as Warwick, prowling the jungle and hunting for ganks. Shania was holding mid lane as Quinn, fearless and sharp — because of course she was.

What began as a few well-timed ganks turned into late-night duos, voice chats that stretched into sunrise, and the kind of connection you can't find in any item shop. He brought the engage; she brought the carries. Together, they were unbeatable.

From summoner names to real names, from screens to in-person, from "GG" to "I love you" — League brought them together, but love is the game they chose to play forever.

And on May 16th, 2026, they'll make it official.
